# DARK SEVEN — BOOK 3: BITTER WATERS
Scripture: Exodus 15:23 / Ruth 1:20
Story Weight: 2007–2008
Status: FRAME BUILT

## Wound Map
Five states away. The married man. Shane's own sister. The theft of Kai. I never get Kai back — carried forward from Book 2, detonated here. Pure, unwashed bitterness. No WHITE in this book. None earned.

## Three-Ink Law
- BLACK — every panel. The long drive back to nothing. The empty apartment. The silence.
- RED — used precisely. The moment the door closes on Kai. One cut. Horizontal. Final.
- WHITE — does not appear.

## SCENE: MCKIBBON HOSPITALITY — BACK IN ROCKET VALLEY (Apr 2007)
He came back from The Angels in February 2007.
April. McKibbon Hospitality. Event Coordinator, Huntsville.
He put his head down. He worked. He kept moving because he had to see his son and seeing his son required being present and being present required a paycheck.
The hallway was quiet for a minute.
Then the floor gave way.

## SCENE: SHE GETS BACK WITH THE EX
She got back with her ex.
They had a kid together.
He watched it happen from whatever distance she allowed, which was always less than he needed and more than he could afford to lose.

## SCENE: FIVE STATES AWAY
After that she took off.
With his kid too.
Five states away. [KEEPER]
Into a married man's arms.
A married man who had been told to divorce Shane's own sister.
(RED — one horizontal line. The cut. The door.)

## SCENE: THE LOCKED DOOR
He tried.
Phone calls, letters, court filings, money sent into the void.
The door didn't just shut.
It was welded from the outside. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: I NEVER GET KAI BACK
Four words.
I never get Kai back. [KEEPER]
Not said in a moment of weakness. Said because it is true, and true things don't soften with time, they just get heavier.
He carried it forward. He still carries it. It is the wound under every other wound in the series.
(BLACK panel. No figure. No horizon. Just black.)

## CLOSE
No rescue. No resolution. No WHITE.
The man is still standing because that's the only thing he knows how to do.
But he is not okay.
The bitterness is real and it is honest and it does not get dressed up in this book.
Exodus 15:23 — They could not drink its water because it was bitter.
He drank it anyway. Because there was nothing else.
