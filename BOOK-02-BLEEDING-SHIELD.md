## SCENE: THE CELTIC CROSS (2005)
He sat in the chair and let them press the ink into the wound.
Not over healed skin. Over the scar that still remembered the floor. The cross went on raw. The shield went on a man still bleeding.
That is what makes it a shield, not a decoration. [KEEPER]
(RED — the ink needle traces the cross line. Covered. Not gone.)
The date on the calendar was the due date of the child lost in The Show Me State. The cross is a grave marker worn on a living man's body. Both wounds. At once.

## SCENE: THE ANGELS — ARRIVAL / THE MANSION (Aug 2005)
Two days after he landed. The job offer. The mansion. Both at once.
Craig Wright Interior Design & Architecture. West Hollywood. Celebrity clients. Executive Assistant.
He lived with Tapioca under that roof. Blood Brother by Blood Shed.
The door at the Rainbow was heavy wood and loud brass. B-Real stood at the threshold checking licenses. Inside, Chyna. They made out in the back shadow of the room.
Then Luke Morgan's place. Then Santa Monica. Then Riverside. If you weren't Vargas, the pavement had an edge. He tucked tail. Front bumper pointed east toward Alabama.

## SCENE: LEGENDS MMA / THE VBC FIGHT
Legends opened on La Brea. He walked through the door the day it unlocked.
Chris Reilly. Bas Rutten. Randy Couture. Rampage Jackson. Mayhem Miller.
Red-eye east. Von Braun Center. Main arena. Third-to-last fight on the card. He fought. Then he flew west again.
