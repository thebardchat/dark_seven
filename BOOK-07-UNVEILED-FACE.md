# DARK SEVEN — BOOK 7: UNVEILED FACE
Scripture: 2 Corinthians 3:18 / Revelation 21:23
Story Weight: 2026 / The Present
Status: PRESSED — THE SERIES CLOSE
Alt title pending: Living Light
First drafted: August 21, 2026 — two days before 1,000 days sober.

## Wound Map
THE LAST WITNESS. The present storm. Tiffany's surgery. The dispatch board. Jaxton at the altar. The WHITE INK BLOWOUT. Dr. Seen hands him the key. Standing on water. I will never take my eyes off of You.

## Three-Ink Law — Series Finale
- BLACK — the storm. Builds to the blowout.
- RED — every wound carried forward from Books 1-6. Then consumed.
- WHITE — THE BLOWOUT. Absolute. Total. Earned by seven books.

## SCENE: THE PRESENT DAY DOESN'T ASK IF YOU'RE TIRED
The present day doesn't ask if you're tired.
It just piles the timber higher on the fire. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: THE STORM IN THE KITCHEN
Tiffany recovering from surgery. The dispatch board humming — eighty concrete operators in the thick Alabama summer heat. Bills stacked like debts that knew his name. Old memories walking the perimeter fence at night, testing the locks. [KEEPER]
Every pressure point tightening in the same hour.
As if the years had been waiting for one single moment to collect everything at once. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: A MAN CAN STAND SOBER FOR A THOUSAND DAYS
A man can stand sober for a thousand days.
He can carry five sons on his back.
He can keep eighty operators rolling and the roof from leaking.
And still look up to find the sky black from one horizon to the other. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: THE CARRY SEQUENCE
You carry the 1996 floor. (RED)
You carry the box cutter and the blood and the scar that became the spine of every book that came after.
You carry the courtrooms, the Show Me State, the last call with David.
Drink one for me. For sure, brother. Love you, man. I love you too, brother. [KEEPER]
You carry the iron door on Kai.
You carry every mile from the West Hollywood mansion back to Rocket Valley.
At some point the knuckles turn white from holding the wheel so hard. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: THE OLD WIRING
The body still knows how to stay ready even when readiness is no longer required. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: THE VOICE CUTS THROUGH THE LIGHTNING
It doesn't matter now. [KEEPER]
Not defeat. Not quitting. Surrender.
Lay every broken piece down at the feet of Jesus Christ and stop pretending you were ever strong enough to carry it alone. [KEEPER]
It doesn't matter what the dark tried to build. It doesn't matter what was stolen. It doesn't matter how long the hallway was or how many times the lights went out. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: JAXTON AT THE ALTAR
No pretense. No hesitation.
Salvation taking root in real time, right in front of his father's eyes.
The covenant breaking through the bloodline like light through a cracked wall. [KEEPER]
The same blood that had carried the scar and the cross and the wreckage now carrying something the dark could not touch. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: THE WHITE INK BLOWOUT
Not today.
The ink breaks the frame.
A blinding, violent, absolute WHITE INK BLOWOUT detonates across the two-page spread.
It does not politely fade the darkness. It does not negotiate. It swallows it whole.
It swallows the 1996 scar. The neon. The Wild Cherry. The Show Me State. Riverside. The mansion. The La Brea mats. The Rainbow.
It consumes every shadow that followed the boy from Rocket Valley into manhood and tried to convince him the hallway would never end. [KEEPER]
The veil is torn top to bottom.
The Celtic Cross is no longer just a shield. It is finished.
The boy is saved. The father is standing in the light.

## SCENE: DR. SEEN — THE KEY
She hands him the key. She disappears.
Her job was never to stay. Her job was to hand him the thing he always had. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: STANDING ON WATER
The door opens. Not into another hallway. Into white.
And he is standing on water. Not sinking. Standing.
Something older than ground — something that already decided to hold. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: THE VOICE — I HAVE ALWAYS BEEN HERE
I have always been here. [KEEPER]
He Who Is Not Afraid.
The One who was in the corridor when the box cutter hit the floor in 1996.
The One in the California wind that said I am now. Always.
The One in the hospital room when Pierce took his first breath.
The One standing behind Jaxton at the altar.
The One who was never surprised by any of it.

## SCENE: THE MAN LOOKS UP — THE CLOSE OF THE SERIES
I will never take my eyes off of You.
Jesus.
I will never take my eyes off of You. [KEEPER — last spoken line of the series]
The Celtic Cross on his back catches the white and throws it back.
Not a shield anymore.
A beacon. [KEEPER]

2 Corinthians 3:18 — And we all, with unveiled face, beholding the glory of the Lord, are being transformed.
He Who Is Not Afraid was in the room from the beginning.
The darkness was never an absence of His jurisdiction.
It was the valley He was already walking through.
The veil is torn. The face is unveiled. The light is not arriving.
It was always here.
