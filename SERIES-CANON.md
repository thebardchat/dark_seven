## Celtic Cross — True Origin
The cross is a grave marker worn on a living man's body. It covers the 1996 back wound AND marks the due date of the unborn child lost in The Show Me State. Both. At once. Placed on his back on the date that child should have been born. Not celebration. Not trophy. Grief made permanent.
