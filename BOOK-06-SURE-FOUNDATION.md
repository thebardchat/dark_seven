# DARK SEVEN — BOOK 6: SURE FOUNDATION
Scripture: Isaiah 28:16 / 1 Corinthians 3:11
Story Weight: 2015–2023
Status: PRESSED

## Wound Map
Sundown Janitorial. SRM Concrete. Ryker born. The pastor's dare. November 27, 2023 — the date that does not move.

## Three-Ink Law
- BLACK — the grind. The dispatch chair.
- RED — the old killer, still running. Thin thread. Patient. Not loud.
- WHITE — November 27, 2023. The date alone on a black field.

## SCENE: SUNDOWN JANITORIAL (Apr 2015)
Retail floors give way to industrial tile.
District Manager. Four years of commercial bleach, stripped wax, midnight crews.
The grind kept the feet moving. The household got fed. The walls stayed standing.

## SCENE: SRM CONCRETE — AUGUST 2019
He climbed into the chair and put the headset on.
Fourteen triaxles. Mixer fleets before sunrise. Hard men running heavy iron across hot asphalt under a sky that didn't care if you were tired. [KEEPER]
First job in twenty years that matched the actual RPM inside his head.
The ADHD wasn't the enemy in that chair. It was the engine.
The same nervous system that had once scanned dark rooms for trouble now scanned a living map of trucks and tons. [KEEPER]
For once the wiring fit the work. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: RYKER (AROUND 2021)
Fifth son. Clean hands running the hallway. The kind of noise that doesn't need drowning.
From the outside, the foundation looked set.

## SCENE: THE OLD KILLER, STILL RUNNING
Not the full-throttle scream of the club years. Just the slow, quiet, daily negotiation. The drink at the end of the shift.
(RED — thin horizontal thread bleeding off the right edge.)

## SCENE: THE PASTOR'S DARE
Six months. No drink. Prove you can. [KEEPER]
He took it because walking away would have said the quiet part out loud — that the bottle still had a vote he wasn't ready to admit. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: THE FIRST WEEKS
Friction. The old reflex kept raising its hand. He kept not calling on it.
Six months came and went. The bet was won.
And then he simply didn't pick the glass back up.
Just the quiet, repeated, immovable refusal to pour the next one. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: NOVEMBER 27, 2023 — THE DATE THAT DOES NOT MOVE
November 27, 2023.
The date that does not move. [KEEPER]
The anchor dropped through the floorboards, through the dirt, through thirty years of blood and wreckage, and bit into bedrock.
(WHITE — the date alone on a black field.)

## SCENE: THE CORNERSTONE
The 1996 scar was still there. The Celtic Cross still cut deep.
But the man standing on top of both had stopped running.
It became the ground he was finally willing to stand on without looking for the exit. [KEEPER]

## CLOSE
The cross on his back had been a shield since 2005.
A cornerstone since November 27, 2023.
Isaiah 28:16 — A tested stone, a precious cornerstone for a sure foundation.
Book 7 begins in the storm that followed.
