# DARK SEVEN — BOOK 5: STILL WATERS
Scripture: Psalm 23:2 / Hosea 2:15
Story Weight: 2011–2014
Status: PRESSED

## Wound Map
Walking out of the nightclub into something that might be daylight. PM Entertainment. Tiffany enters November 2012. Jaxton born August 14, 2013. The chaos begins to still.

## Three-Ink Law
- BLACK — the adjustment. The quiet that still has teeth.
- RED — the carrying. Thin thread. Still present.
- WHITE — Jaxton's birth. Earned. The third WHITE of the series.

## SCENE: WALKING OUT OF THE NIGHTCLUB
Walking out of the nightclub didn't make the daylight comfortable.
Mickey's. Club Rush. The Wild Cherry Cabaret. General Manager. Eighty women. Porn stars every two weeks.
When those doors finally closed, the quiet had teeth.

## SCENE: PM ENTERTAINMENT — HARVEST, AL (Apr 2011)
New building. New noise. Concrete floors, arcade cabinets, screaming kids. Family hours. Day shift.
Honest noise. Finite noise.
He tried to teach his hands how to live without the midnight edge.

## SCENE: THE CARRYING
The box-cutter scar. The Celtic Cross. The wind off the ledge. David's ghost. The iron door on Kai. I never get Kai back. The years of club smoke and stage light still clinging to the lining of every jacket he owned. [KEEPER]
Sixteen years of hallways and ambushes will teach a man that the quiet is only temporary. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: NOVEMBER 2012 — TIFFANY WALKS IN
She didn't get the cleaned-up version.
She saw the broken drywall. She saw the caution tape. She saw the shadows in the corners.
And she walked in anyway.
No speeches. No rescue mission.
Something in the air shifted — from survival to the thinner, stranger possibility of staying. [KEEPER]

## SCENE: AUGUST 14, 2013 — JAXTON (EARNED WHITE OF BOOK 5)
Another anchor dropped into the bedrock.
He held the weight of it and felt the floor under his feet stay where it was. [KEEPER]
(WHITE — hospital fluorescents. Clean and total.)

## SCENE: MARCH 2014 — VERIZON WIRELESS, SCOTTSBORO
The same roads he had run in earlier years, now traveled with a different kind of cargo. [KEEPER]
The cross had been a shield since 2005.
For the first time, it was starting to look like something you build on.

## CLOSE
The chaos is not gone. The scars are not gone. But something has stilled.
He is building something on top of the ruins instead of just surviving inside them.
Psalm 23:2 — He leads me beside still waters; He restores my soul.
